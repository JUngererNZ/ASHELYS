About the baker

A portfolio that feels handmade, not overbuilt.

This layout is meant to be affordable, simple to update, and strong enough to act as a polished placeholder until the baker is ready for a bigger website. It gives equal focus to beautiful product imagery, a short personal story, and a direct path for enquiries.
