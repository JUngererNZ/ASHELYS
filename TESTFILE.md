Set on the stunningly peaceful Honeytrails Farm, Rose and Bean is one of those rare places that instantly makes you exhale. Right on the Comrades route, just before Monteseel, it feels like a hidden pause button on the world.

The coffee is genuinely some of the best I’ve ever had — smooth, rich, and perfectly balanced. The cakes? Moist, indulgent, and impossible to resist. Every sweet and savoury option we’ve tried has been excellent, from hearty breakfasts and satisfying lunches to truly special high teas. Nothing has disappointed — not once.

The service is top-notch: friendly, attentive, and unpretentious. And then there’s Paige, the warm, authentic hostess who makes you feel like you’ve popped in to your favourite auntie’s place for tea — welcomed, remembered, and utterly at home.

Add to that a garden shop, nail salon, home crafts and farm stall section, and beautiful gardens where kids and pets can run and play, genuinely great prices, and a setting that invites you to linger far longer than planned… and you have something really special.

Rose and Bean isn’t just a coffee stop — it’s a destination. One you’ll want to return to again and again.
